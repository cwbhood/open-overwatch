What you see

Everything moving.
One screen.

Layers you switch on and off like an air-traffic controller. Click anything for the full picture: callsign, altitude, orbit, magnitude, wind speed, live camera frame.

AIR

Aircraft

Every ADS-B transponder in range, worldwide. Civil, military and emergency squawks, each colour-coded, with trails and type.

SPACE

Satellites

Orbits computed in your browser from CelesTrak data with SGP4: the ISS, Tiangong, weather sats and GPS, with ground tracks.

SEA

Ships

AIS vessel positions: the Baltic out of the box, the whole world's shipping with a free aisstream.io key.

GROUND

Earthquakes

USGS quakes as they're reported, sized by magnitude, plus GDACS disaster alerts and NASA natural events.

WEATHER

Storms

Hurricanes and typhoons with forecast cones, precipitation radar, severe-weather alerts and the aurora forecast.

EYES

Cameras

Public traffic cameras from London, New York, California, Singapore and Finland. Click one for the latest frame.

Get it

Two ways
in.

The web version runs right here. The download adds a tiny local helper. A few feeds (the ADS-B aircraft networks, OpenSky, NOAA hurricane cones, some cameras) refuse requests from web pages, and the helper relays just those.

  1. Download & unzip

    One folder, about 7 MB. Nothing to install.

  2. Double-click the launcher

    Start Open Overwatch.bat on Windows, or python3 serve.py / node serve.js on Mac and Linux.

  3. Watch the world

    Your browser opens the map at 127.0.0.1:8787. Every layer lights up.

Open Overwatch

A hobby project built on public data. For awareness and curiosity, not for navigation, safety or operational decisions.

Data: adsb.lol, adsb.fi, airplanes.live, OpenSky, CelesTrak, USGS, NOAA/NHC/NWS, NASA EONET & FIRMS, GDACS, GDELT, Digitraffic, aisstream.io, SondeHub, RainViewer, TeleGeography. Basemap © Esri. Night lights: NASA Black Marble.

Not for safety or decisions that matter

Use it for curiosity and awareness only. Do not use it for navigation, flying, sailing, driving, emergency response, search and rescue, evacuation, trading, or anything where a late, wrong or missing position could hurt someone or cost money. Always use official, authorised sources for those.

Other people's data can be wrong or faked

Everything shown comes from third parties: public agencies, volunteer networks and open datasets. It can be late, incomplete, out of date or simply wrong. Positions that are broadcast by the transmitter itself (aircraft ADS-B, ship AIS, weather balloons) can be spoofed or entered falsely by anyone. Treat every dot as unverified.

Weather balloons: a specific warning

Balloon (radiosonde) tracks on the 2D map come from SondeHub, an open community service where anyone can upload a balloon's position, name and details, with no check. A balloon on the map can therefore be fake, misplaced, or carry text written to look official or to attack a web page (a script “injection”). The balloon layer is off by default and shows this warning before it can be switched on; the app displays every balloon field as plain text, never runs it as code, and links only to SondeHub. Even so: do not click links or follow instructions found in balloon details elsewhere, and never go looking for a landed balloon because of this map. Switching the layer on sends the centre of your map view to SondeHub.

Privacy

This project runs no accounts, ads, cookies or analytics. Your browser does, however, talk directly to the data providers, a code CDN (jsDelivr, cdnjs) and Google Fonts to load the page and the data, and those services can see your IP address under their own policies. Preferences are saved only in your browser's own storage. “Look up” and “Around me” use your device's location on your device; the app does not send it anywhere, though the map area you view is requested from the data providers.

Links and names

Links lead to other sites that this project does not control or endorse. Names such as NASA, NOAA, USGS, Cesium, Esri, OpenStreetMap, Wikidata and SondeHub belong to their owners; this project is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them. If something here is wrong, or a source wants it changed or removed, please open an issue.
